Jobs

Prepare for a part-time job search

Your preparation checklist

  1. Students need work permission (資格外活動許可) before starting. During school terms the limit is 28 hours a week. Check with your school or immigration office.
  2. Prepare your résumé and weekly availability.
  3. Ask the employer about duties, hours, pay and travel costs.
  4. Read and keep the written employment conditions.

Steps to follow

  1. Confirm you are allowed to work. Students need permission (資格外活動許可) before starting.
  2. Search Hello Work and job sites. Note the duties, hourly pay and nearest station.
  3. Prepare your résumé and write down the hours you can work each week.
  4. Apply and attend the interview. Bring your residence card; bring bank details if you are hired.
  5. Read the written employment conditions before your first shift and keep a copy.

What to bring

  • Résumé (Japanese rirekisho format is common)
  • Residence card, and student ID if you are a student
  • Your weekly availability, written down
  • Bank account details, for your salary after you are hired
