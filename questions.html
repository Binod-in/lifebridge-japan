Questions people often ask

A starting point for your next conversation with an office, school or provider.

How do I open a bank account?

Requirements depend on the bank and your situation. Ask the bank which documents it accepts before visiting.

What should I do after moving?

Check address procedures with your city office and update your utilities and service providers.

Can international students work part-time?

Only with permission (資格外活動許可). During school terms the limit is 28 hours a week. Confirm your conditions with your school or immigration office.
