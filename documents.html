Documents

Organize your residence and address paperwork

Your preparation checklist

  1. Keep your identification and important documents organized.
  2. Register your address at the city or ward office within 14 days of moving in.
  3. Confirm the required documents and deadlines directly with the office.
  4. Keep a record of appointments and completed procedures.

Steps to follow

  1. Find your city or ward office and ask which counter handles moving-in (転入届, tennyu-todoke).
  2. Fill in the moving-in form at the office. Show your residence card if you need help.
  3. Submit the form with your residence card and passport. Your new address is printed on the back of the card.
  4. Ask about health insurance and pension enrollment during the same visit.
  5. Keep every receipt and letter you receive, and save your next deadlines in Important Dates.

What to bring

  • Residence card (在留カード)
  • Passport
  • Proof of your new address, such as the rental contract
  • Your personal seal (hanko) if you have one. A signature is often accepted, so ask the office.
