Housing

Apartments, moving and utilities

Your preparation checklist

  1. Write down your budget, preferred area and commute.
  2. Ask the agent for a written breakdown of initial and monthly costs.
  3. Confirm utilities, internet and move-in arrangements.
  4. Make a list of places that need your updated address.

Steps to follow

  1. Decide your budget first. Initial costs often equal 4 to 6 months of rent (deposit, key money, agent fee).
  2. Search rooms on several sites and visit in person. Check daylight, noise, storage and the walk to the station.
  3. Ask the agent for a written breakdown of initial costs, monthly rent, renewal fees and move-out rules.
  4. Sign the contract and arrange electricity, gas, water and internet before moving day.
  5. After moving in, register your address at the ward office within 14 days and update your bank, employer and post office.

What to bring

  • Residence card and passport
  • Proof of income or employment, such as a pay slip or offer letter
  • Japanese bank account details, for rent payments
  • Emergency contact details in Japan, if the agent asks
