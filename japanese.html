Japanese phrase helper

Read, listen, copy and save a phrase for your next conversation.

Steps to follow

  1. Pick one category above, such as Hospital or City Hall, and read the phrase of the day.
  2. Press Listen and repeat out loud several times until it feels natural.
  3. Press Copy to paste the phrase into a message or translation app when you need it.
  4. Save phrases you will reuse. Review them in Saved Phrases before you go out.
  5. Use one new phrase in a real conversation every day. Short and polite is enough.

What to bring

  • A notebook or phone app, to write down new words
  • Headphones, so you can listen and repeat anywhere
  • One phrase from this helper, to try today
  • Your Saved Phrases list, to review before you go out
