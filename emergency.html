Emergency help

In Japan, use the emergency numbers below for urgent assistance.

Police · 110

For an urgent police emergency.

Call 110

Ambulance / Fire · 119

For an ambulance or a fire emergency.

Call 119

Not sure it is urgent? · #7119

In some areas, including Tokyo and Saitama, #7119 gives advice on whether you need an ambulance or which hospital to visit. It is not available everywhere, so check your prefecture. If someone may be in danger, call 119.

Call #7119

Japan Visitor Hotline

JNTO's Japan Visitor Hotline offers tourist assistance 24 hours a day in English, Chinese and Korean.

050-3816-2787

Lost phone

Contact the place or transport operator where you last had it. Ask your mobile provider about protecting your service.

Lost bank card

Contact your bank through its official website or app for its lost-card procedure.

Lost residence card

Report the loss to the police and keep the report number they give you. Then apply for a new card at your regional immigration office within 14 days.

Steps to follow

  1. Move to a safe place first. Stay calm and check who needs help.
  2. Call 110 for police or 119 for fire and ambulance. Both work from any phone.
  3. Say your location clearly. Keep your address written in Japanese for this moment.
  4. Follow the operator's instructions and stay on the line until they hang up.
  5. Afterwards, contact your family, school or employer, and keep any report numbers.

What to bring

  • Your address written in Japanese, kept in your wallet
  • Your health insurance card, carried with you
  • A charged phone, with emergency numbers saved
  • Your residence card, for identification after an incident
