Transportation

Plan your route and learn to use local transport

Your preparation checklist

  1. Look up your route, destination and final connection.
  2. Check which payment methods the operator accepts.
  3. Ask station staff about IC cards and commuter passes.
  4. Keep your destination written in Japanese for easy reference.

Steps to follow

  1. Look up your route the night before. Note the last train time if you go out late.
  2. Get an IC card (Suica, Pasmo or similar) at a station ticket machine or service counter.
  3. If you commute daily, ask station staff about a commuter pass (teikiken) for your route.
  4. Keep your destination written in Japanese and follow station signs in English.
  5. If you are lost, show your destination to station staff or use a route search app.

What to bring

  • Charged IC card, with auto-charge if your card supports it
  • Small cash or coins, for ticket machines and rural buses
  • Your destination written in Japanese
  • Your commuter pass or student ID, if you have one
