Money

Prepare for banking and everyday expenses

Your preparation checklist

  1. Choose a bank and check its requirements for your situation.
  2. Ask which identification, address and contact details are needed.
  3. Compare fees and available language support.
  4. Plan a monthly budget for rent, food, transport and bills.

Steps to follow

  1. Compare a few banks. Ask about monthly fees, ATM hours, English support and how to receive your salary.
  2. Open an account with your residence card, personal seal or signature, and phone number.
  3. Set up automatic payments for rent, utilities and phone so you never miss a deadline.
  4. List your monthly income and fixed costs, then write a simple budget with the Money Tracker.
  5. Keep tax and pension letters in one folder. Ask your employer or ward office if anything is unclear.

What to bring

  • Residence card
  • Personal seal (hanko) or a signature, as the bank requires
  • Proof of address, such as your residence card back side or a utility bill
  • Your phone, for verification codes and banking apps
