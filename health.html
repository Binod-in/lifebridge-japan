Health

Prepare for appointments and insurance questions

Your preparation checklist

  1. Ask your city office, school or employer about applicable health coverage.
  2. Find a nearby clinic and check its opening hours and language support.
  3. Ask the clinic what to bring before your appointment.
  4. Write down your symptoms, medicines and questions to discuss with a professional.

Steps to follow

  1. Enroll in health insurance: employees through their company, others through National Health Insurance at the ward office.
  2. Find a nearby clinic. Check opening hours, how to book, and what languages are supported.
  3. Before your visit, write down your symptoms, medicines and questions.
  4. Bring your insurance card and arrive a little early for first-visit paperwork.
  5. After the visit, keep receipts and follow the treatment instructions you received.

What to bring

  • Health insurance card
  • Residence card or other ID
  • Your symptom and medicine notes
  • Cash or card, for the part of the cost you pay yourself
